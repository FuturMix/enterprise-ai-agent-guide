## Cover Letter

Dear Hiring Team,

I am writing to express my interest in the backend developer position. With six years of experience spanning both consumer-facing and enterprise B2B platforms, I offer a unique perspective on building backend systems that serve vastly different user expectations, scale patterns, and business requirements.

### Why I Am a Strong Fit

My career began in B2C, where I built high-concurrency Java services powering a consumer marketplace with 2 million monthly active users. I learned to optimize for latency, handle unpredictable traffic spikes, and design user-facing APIs that prioritize speed and simplicity. That foundation gave me strong instincts for performance tuning and resilient system design.

Three years ago, I made a deliberate pivot to B2B enterprise software. This transition taught me an entirely new set of challenges: multi-tenancy, role-based access control, complex billing models, and the long sales cycles that demand rock-solid reliability. I led the backend rebuild of our core platform using Kotlin and Spring Boot, introducing a modular architecture that allowed us to onboard enterprise clients with custom configurations without forking the codebase.

### The Value of the Pivot

Engineers who have only worked in B2C or only in B2B tend to over-index on the patterns they know. My experience across both worlds gives me the judgment to know when consumer-grade speed matters and when enterprise-grade configurability takes priority. I can design APIs that are both developer-friendly and compliance-ready.

### What I Bring to This Team

- **Cross-domain fluency:** Deep experience in both B2C speed and B2B complexity.
- **JVM mastery:** Expert-level Java and Kotlin, with extensive Spring Boot and Spring Cloud experience.
- **Architecture for flexibility:** Proven ability to build multi-tenant systems that scale without sacrificing customization.
- **Clear technical writing:** I document architectural decisions, write detailed RFCs, and ensure knowledge is shared across the team.

I am looking for a role where my cross-domain experience is valued and where I can help build backend systems that serve enterprise customers without losing the agility of a product-driven team.

Thank you for your consideration.
